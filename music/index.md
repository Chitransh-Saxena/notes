# Guitar

Songs I'm learning, theory I keep forgetting, and practice that actually worked.

Most guitar theory gets taught twice — once in Western terms and once in sargam —
and almost never in a way that shows they are the same system. This series does
that, because I grew up hearing **sa re ga ma** and learning **C D E F**, and it
took far too long to notice they describe the same thing.

## The one thing worth knowing first

**Sargam is movable.** Sa is not a fixed pitch — it is whichever note you decide
to call home. Choose C and you are in C major; choose D and every relationship
stays identical, just higher. Western solfège calls this *movable do*, and it is
the same idea.

That is why the fret patterns in [[Guitar — Sargam and the scale]] work anywhere
on the neck. Learn the shape once and you have it in all twelve keys — and it is
why [[Guitar — The capo]] works at all: a capo does nothing but choose a
different Sa.
