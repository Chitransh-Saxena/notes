## The one-paragraph version

Buy a radio, not a drone. Put twenty hours into a simulator before you spend
anything else. Then a cheap whoop indoors, then a 5" — bought or built. Learn to
solder before you learn to tune. Everything else is detail, and the detail is in
the notes above.

> [!warning] Rules before rotors
> Airspace rules are local, they change, and they are your responsibility. In
> India that means DGCA and the Digital Sky portal; elsewhere it is someone else.
> Check the current rules yourself — do not take a note written months ago,
> including this one, as authority.
